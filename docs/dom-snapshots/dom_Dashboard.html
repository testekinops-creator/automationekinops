RMA Requests

RMA Dashboard
The RMA Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of all RMA requests, enabling real-time tracking of RMA status across various stages and supporting efficient monitoring and follow-up.
RMA Repair In Progress
11

RMA requests where the device has been received and is currently undergoing repair activities but has not yet been completed.
RMA - Pending Accept
9

RMA requests that have been submitted but are still awaiting review and approval by the service or support team.
RMA - In Progress More Than 30 Days
0

RMA requests where the device has been received or is on hold and has remained in progress for over 30 days without completion, requiring attention or follow-up.

RMA - Accepted & Not Received
0

RMA requests that have been approved, but the device has not been received at the service facility even after 30 days.

Open RMA - Submitted More Than 3 Times
5

This is the RMA requests where the same customer or device has had an RMA request in the past more than three times.
RMA - Repaired But Not Closed
9

RMA requests where the repair or resolution has been completed, but the case has not yet been formally closed in the system.

Factory Receive

RMA Factory Receive Form
The Factory Receive RMA page is used to record the devices received at the factory for repair. This section allows the repair engineers to verify RMA details and update the receipt status of products. Once a product or serial number (S/N) status is updated to “Received,” an email notification will be sent to the customer informing them that Ekinops has received the device for repair. Devices can be received only against RMA requests in the accepted status. For all other cases, please contact your supervisor.
